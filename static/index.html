THE BRIEF

Most days the market is noise. A handful of days each month, it moves like a stampede — rate decisions, expiries, overnight US shocks, heavyweight earnings. This engine sleeps on ordinary days and only wakes for those. Here is exactly what happens on a hunt day.

THE WAITING GAMEThe wolf doesn't chase. It marks the first 15 minutes, draws the range, and lets the market walk into the open.
08:30
The morning brief
Autopilot reads the calendar, overnight headlines and India VIX, then grades the day A / B / C. A = full risk. B = half. C — the wolf stays in the trees.
09:07
Auto-arm
If the day passed, the engine arms itself. Practice mode needs nothing. Live needs a fresh token + your confirmation.
09:15
The impulse range
First 15 minutes get measured. A range under 0.15% = chop — no trade, wolf walks away.
BREAK
The strike
Price breaks the range AND the news agrees → buy the ATM option. Calls above the range, puts below. News disagrees → vetoed.
MANAGE
Stop · TP1 · Runner
Stop at −55% of premium. Book 40% at 2×. The rest runs for 25× or trails — this runner is where the big money lives.
15:20
Flatten. Always.
Everything closes. No overnight holds, ever. The result lands in the record the same evening.
